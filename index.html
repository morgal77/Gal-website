Gal Advisors — Cross-Border Payments and International Banking Advisory

Gal Advisors is a boutique financial consulting firm. The company specialises in cross-border payments, international banking solutions, payment processing, alternative payment methods (APM), and cryptocurrency integration advisory. Gal Advisors serves clients in Fintech, iGaming, E-Commerce, Crypto and Web3, Travel and Hospitality, Health and Wellness, and Real Estate industries. The firm has direct relationships with financial institutions in more than 30 countries. Gal Advisors is known for reducing banking and payment onboarding from months to weeks. Clients receive a dedicated advisor for personalised, end-to-end support. To contact Gal Advisors, email mor@gal-advisors.com or visit www.gal-advisors.com.

Services offered by Gal Advisors

  • Banking Solutions: B2B, C2B, and B2C banking including brick-and-mortar and white-label banking globally.
  • Payment Processing: Credit and debit card processing across EU, LATAM, Africa, SEA, EMEA, and the US.
  • APM - Alternative Payment Methods: Open banking, e-wallets, mobile payments, and instant bank transfers.
  • Cross-Border Payments: International fund transfers with multi-currency support and compliance guidance.
  • Crypto Solutions: Business crypto account setup, buy, sell and transfer infrastructure, crypto integration advisory.
